<h1 align="center">Open tools for artists</h1>

<p align="center">Small, free tools for the practical side of making art.<br>They run in your browser, need no account, and never upload your work.</p>

<p align="center"><a href="https://olya-haydamaka.github.io">olya-haydamaka.github.io</a> · <a href="https://haydamaka.com">haydamaka.com</a></p>

---

### Tools

**[Postcard fronts](https://github.com/olya-haydamaka/postcard-fronts)** · [open tool](https://postcards.haydamaka.com/)
Place paintings on postcard fronts and backs, check bleed, trim and resolution, and export print-ready 300 dpi TIFF files.

### Principles

- **Free:** MIT licence, for personal or commercial work.
- **Private:** your files stay on your computer.
- **Simple:** one task per tool, no sign-up.
